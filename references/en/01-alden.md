My name is Jinwoo Kang (Alden), and I lead the Infrastructure Division at Karrot. I hired Taehyun Lee (Walter) as an intern at Karrot in January 2023, and we worked together for nearly three years and five months until he left in May 2026 to pursue graduate studies in the United States. ==I chose Taehyun for the winter internship because of his potential for growth and positive attitude, and he exceeded my expectations on both counts.=={성장}

For three years, Taehyun was responsible for internal platforms used every day by multiple product teams, including video and image platforms and tools for monitoring service health. The video platform in particular had to be built from the ground up. ==His flexible design avoided dependence on a particular SaaS provider, and his reliable operation of the platform enabled the successful launch of Karrot Story.=={설계}

==In just three years, these qualities helped Taehyun grow from an L3 to an L4 engineer.=={성장} Had he not wanted to pursue his dream of graduate school, I would have continued to support him in staying at Karrot and building better platforms. He was such a strong engineer that I did not want him to leave. I was sorry to see him go, but supported his decision to pursue his dream.

I am confident that Taehyun will continue to learn quickly and soon become an engineer of a very high caliber.
